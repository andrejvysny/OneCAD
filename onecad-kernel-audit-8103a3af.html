A. Executive summary

Modeling kernel

Strong on current authoring paths. About 250 direct probes against the real worker matched closed-form volume and area to roughly 1e-16 relative error (worst case 2.4e-9, an oblique loft). Refusals were explicit and named. I found no case where an invalid B-Rep was accepted as success.

Assembly engine

The transform, residual and degree-of-freedom math is correct at millimetre scale. Usability of the solver is not: contact mates from picked faces fail or flip the part, convergence breaks at lever arms of 0.3–1 m, and driving one joint moves others.

Most serious correctness risks

  1. The worker crashes with a segmentation fault on a revolve whose profile sits about 1e-7 mm off the axis.
  2. A metadata-only edit (rename, colour, visibility) cancels the regeneration in flight and nothing schedules a replacement.
  3. Joints built from picked faces cannot express a contact mate. The solve fails, or converges with the part upside-down inside its mate.
  4. The joint solver fails on trivially solvable single joints once lever arms reach roughly 0.3–1 m.

What was verified by execution

  • 247 of 247 C++ worker tests.
  • 2655 Rust tests across core, assembly, protocol, app, regen, kernelbench and xtask, with 0 failures, against the real worker.
  • 6 ignored real-worker tests, the 3 xtask modeling gates, and the solver repeatability test.
  • Bit-identical results across 6 fresh worker processes, and a STEP export→import round trip.
  • About 250 analytic probes and 4 added reproduction tests (in a scratch copy, not in the repository).

Readiness for dependable macOS part and small-assembly modeling

Part modeling is close; risks 1 and 2 block it. Small-assembly modeling is not dependable yet because of risks 3 and 4 and the drive side effect (finding 5). Native UI workflows, GPU display and appearance were not exercised at all.

B. Findings ranked by severity

Confirmed means reproduced by execution in this audit. Strong evidence means the code path was read end to end but not executed. Hypothesis means the outcome is inferred. Reproduction scripts are listed in section E.

P1 — high

P1 · 1

Revolve with a near-axis profile crashes the worker

Confirmed
Where
worker/src/ops/RevolveOp.cpp:423 (crossing classifier dead zone) and :443 (unguarded BRepPrimAPI_MakeRevol)
Subsystem
Revolve, all boolean modes
Reproduce
Sketch a rectangle x∈[1e-7, 10], y∈[0, 10] with a construction axis on x=0. Revolve 360° or 90°. Either sign of the gap triggers it. Script: p4.py.
Expected
A named refusal, or a snap to the axis, for any profile point closer than the authoring resolution (1e-3 mm) that is not exactly on the axis.
Observed
EXC_BAD_ACCESS (SIGSEGV) in Extrema_ExtCC::Points ← BRepPrimAPI_MakeRevol::CheckValidity ← revolve_impl. Neighbouring gaps behave inconsistently:
  • ≤ 1e-8: treated as on-axis, 3 faces, correct volume.
  • 1e-7: crash.
  • 1e-6: GEOMETRY_INVALID.
  • 1e-5 to 5e-4: accepted with a 10–500 nm bore along the axis (4 faces).
  • −1e-4: generic "Revolve operation failed" instead of REVOLVE_PROFILE_CROSSES_AXIS.
Root cause
The classifier only refuses when the profile reaches beyond ±resolution on both sides. A point inside the band but off the axis flows into OCCT, whose validity check dereferences an empty extrema result when an edge is parallel to the axis at about Precision::Confusion(). A segfault is not a C++ exception, so the catch (...) at :451 cannot contain it.
Fix
Add a preflight at :423: every profile point with |offset| < resolution must be on the axis within confusion, otherwise snap it or refuse with a new REVOLVE_PROFILE_NEAR_AXIS.
P1 · 2

A metadata-only edit supersedes the regeneration in flight and nothing reschedules it

Mechanism confirmed · UI hang not executed
Where
crates/onecad-app/src/document_runtime.rs:1455 (after_mutation runs for RegenHint::None edits), :2644-2668 (failure relabelled as superseded); crates/onecad-core/src/regen/scheduler.rs:569 (no action on Superseded)
Subsystem
Regeneration scheduling, document runtime
Reproduce
promotable_fake_runtime() → apply(add_extrude(0x11, 10.0)) → begin_regen(ToEnd{from:1}) → apply(RenameBody) → finish_regen(drive). Test audit1_… in the scratch copy.
Expected
A regeneration covering the extrude eventually publishes two bodies.
Observed
The rename returns RegenHint::None, the regeneration reports Superseded, the projection still has 1 body, and nothing is queued.
Impact
From code reading: the edit's geometry is never published; if the worker already accepted, the worker head and the Rust head diverge until the next geometric edit, so picks and fenced exports return stale; a hard failure in that window is relabelled Superseded and lost; the UI's wait has no timeout. Rename body or sketch, visibility, body or face colour, module state and add-sketch all take this path.
Fix
Do not bump the fencing revision for edits that need no regeneration, or re-request the superseded span when a regeneration ends Superseded with no successor.
P1 · 3

Joint origins from picked faces cannot describe a contact mate

Solver confirmed · pick path read
Where
crates/onecad-app/src/api/component_execution/joint_origin.rs:174-195 (z = outward face normal or cylinder axis, no flip); crates/onecad-assembly/src/solver.rs:526-541 (all joint kinds require both z axes to point the same way)
Subsystem
Assembly joints: rigid, revolute, slider
Reproduce
Frame A: origin (0,0,10), z=+Z (top face). Frame B: origin 0, z=−Z (bottom face). A grounded at identity, B free at identity (the insert default). Script: asmprobe.
Expected
B seated upright on A at z=10.
Observed
  • Rigid and slider from identity: NotConverged, residual π.
  • Revolute from identity or with a 1e-3 rad roll: NotConverged, residual 2.
  • Any start that does converge puts B's local +Z at (0,0,−1): B is flipped and sits inside A.
Root cause
Outward normals of two touching faces are opposed, but the joint has no flip flag (the library mate has one), and a bound frame must equal the resolved frame exactly, so the user cannot negate it. The exact-π start is a stationary point of the rotation residual.
Fix
Add a flip or contact option per joint side, and perturb or solve in closed form when the start is antiparallel.

P2 · 4

Joint solver does not converge at large lever arms

Confirmed
Where
crates/onecad-assembly/src/solver.rs:36-50: finite-difference Levenberg–Marquardt, 50 iterations, unknowns in unscaled radians and millimetres. The app uses these defaults (document_runtime/joint_execution.rs:197).
Reproduce
One grounded body, one free body, one joint with a closed-form answer. Scale frame offsets and positions by s.
Observed
  • Frame offsets about 0.3 m, start a few metres away: rigid and slider NotConverged.
  • Offsets about 1 m: a 5° and 1 mm perturbation needs 47 of 50 iterations.
  • Offsets about 3 m: the same perturbation fails.
  • At s=1 everything converges in 4–7 iterations with residual below 4e-10.
Expected
Convergence in a few iterations at any scale; the answer is B = A·la·J(value)·lb⁻¹.
Note
The failure is honest: no poses are returned, so nothing moves.
Fix
For grounded trees, compute poses by closed-form propagation and use them as the answer or the initial guess. Scale rotation columns by a characteristic length.
P2 · 5

Driving one joint moves other free joints, and their stored value goes stale

Solver confirmed · persistence read
Where
solver.rs:256-267 (only joints in the drive list are pinned); crates/onecad-app/src/joint_execution.rs:235-240 (only driven joints' value is rewritten)
Reproduce
Ground G, bodies B1 and B2 at z=2, slider J1 (G→B1, value 2), slider J2 (B1→B2). Drive J2 = 5 only.
Expected
B1 stays at z=2, B2 moves to z=7.
Observed
B1 moves to z=−0.5 and B2 to z=4.5. J1's stored value remains 2.0.
Fix
Pin undriven joints at their stored values during a drive, or write back every joint's measured value at commit.

Latest-wins coalescing drops the cancelled regeneration's edit claim

Claim loss confirmed · wrong bind is a hypothesis
Where
crates/onecad-core/src/regen/scheduler.rs:504-535
Reproduce

P2 · 11

A stale mesh fetch silently retries at the worker's current head

Strong evidence
Where
crates/onecad-app/src/worker/manager.rs:2903-2925; document_runtime.rs:375-398, :3154-3166; export_threemf.rs:96
Mechanism
On STALE_PREVIEW the fetch re-reads the worker head and returns those bytes without saying which snapshot they belong to. Adoption checks against the requested snapshot id, so bytes from another head are cached under the current generation. The 3MF export resolves face colours against snapshot S and then fetches through the same retry, so a publish mid-export can colour the wrong faces.
Fix
Return the snapshot id with the bytes and refuse a mismatch; never retry at a fresh head for fenced consumers.
P2 · 12

Worker snapshot ids restart at 1 after a worker restart

Strong evidence
Where
worker/src/session/Session.cpp:166, :193, :391; document_runtime.rs:5492-5494, :5583-5627; document_runtime/staged_elements.rs:51-73
Mechanism
Rust caches and fences that key on the bare snapshot id treat a post-restart snapshot as the pre-restart one with the same number. An exact-key promotion-cache hit can reuse an old element id for a different face. User impact is a hypothesis.
Fix
Key every snapshot cache and fence on (worker epoch, snapshot id), or clear them in the restart hook.
P2 · 13

The host deletes a valid new feature when the regeneration failed for transport reasons

Strong evidence
Where
crates/onecad-ui/src/provisional.rs:198-222; document_runtime.rs:2651-2668; worker/manager.rs:2056-2062
Mechanism
A commit during the restart backoff window fails with NotConnected; a crash gives Crashed or Protocol. The report says failed, and should_rollback does not look at the error class, so it spends the rollback token and undoes the user's feature. Combined with finding 1, a crash-triggering revolve would be removed rather than reported.
Fix
Classify failures as availability or feature errors; only the latter are eligible for provisional rollback.
P2 · 14

Stream reassembly trusts the worker's byte offset

Strong evidence · code read directly
Where
crates/onecad-app/src/worker/manager.rs:2294-2299
Mechanism
StreamAcc::apply resizes its buffer to byteOffset + len with no bound against totalBytes or a cap. A single bad chunk can force a huge allocation, and off + bin.len() can overflow. Either defeats the sidecar's crash isolation by taking the host down.
Fix
Validate each data chunk against the manifest's totalBytes and the 1 GiB frame cap with checked arithmetic; treat a violation as a protocol error.
P2 · 15

STEP import can drop open shells and loose faces without a loss diagnostic

Strong evidence · root layout is a hypothesis
Where
worker/src/io/StepRead.cpp:295-311, :524-531
Mechanism
promote_solids walks solids and shells only. An open-shell flag is reported only when the root produced zero solids, so a surface part next to solid parts in one assembly root disappears silently.
Fix
Count skipped shells and free faces per root and emit a diagnostic naming them, or quarantine them as bodies.
P2 · 16

Picked joint origins sit at the surface's parametric origin and cannot be offset

Strong evidence · concrete positions are a hypothesis
Where
worker/src/session/ClassifyElement.cpp:115-124; joint_origin.rs:191-192
Mechanism
A plane's origin is gp_Pln::Location() and a cylinder's is its axis location. Neither needs to lie on the face. Rigid and revolute joints put origins together, so in-plane position and axial seating depend on how each body happened to be modelled. Editing a bound origin breaks the exact-frame match and triggers NeedsRepair.
Fix
Use the face centroid or a user-picked point projected onto the surface, and allow an authored offset.

D. Assembly and solver validation

Sound, by execution

  • Rigid, revolute and slider residuals below 4e-10 at millimetre scale, from scrambled starts.
  • Drive sign is consistent: revolute angle and slider travel match the commanded value across ±π.
  • Limits: a start outside limits is clamped to the nearest bound; a drive outside limits is refused; a value outside limits is invalid.
  • Degrees of freedom: chain of two revolutes and a slider = 3; unanchored revolute pair = 7; free body = 6. Redundant counts are correct (duplicate rigid = 6).
  • Grounded poses are returned bit-exact. Repeat runs are bit-identical; reordered input differs by 1.8e-14 mm.
  • NaN poses, non-unit quaternions, degenerate frames and self-joints are rejected.
  • At crate level a driven planar 4-bar loop closes to 1.3e-10 with the right mobility.

Sound, by code reading (not executed)

  • Hamilton wxyz quaternions; unit norm enforced at deserialize, insert, solver input and export.
  • Composition is parent∘child; inverse is (q*, −q*·t); frames are right-handed with y = z×x.
  • Commit is fenced on session, generation, revision, epoch, snapshot and base hash.
  • Solved poses are stored and not re-solved at open, so reopening does not move parts.
  • STEP export writes each visible root occurrence once; gp_Trsf = T·R matches RigidTransform; scale is 1; output is flattened as documented.

G. Final verdict

Demonstrably reliable

  • Extrude, boolean, fillet, chamfer, hole, mirror, patterns and transform on current authoring paths.
  • Shell on bodies without through holes; loft and sweep within their declared scope.
  • Publication gating: invalid, empty, disjoint and no-effect-cut results are refused.
  • Worker-level failure atomicity and cross-process determinism.
  • STEP solid round trip.
  • Joint math at millimetre scale for grounded trees.

Unverified

  • Native UI workflows, GPU display and appearance.
  • OffsetFace, Gear, FeaturePattern and components beyond the repository's own tests.
  • Edit-driven reference rebinding, undo/redo and save/reopen equivalence beyond the Rust suites.
  • Crash recovery end to end.
  • Findings 11–16 and 30–37, and the clear-publish claim.

Unsafe or incomplete

  • Revolve near the axis.
  • Regeneration scheduling around metadata edits and coalescing.
  • Contact mates from picked faces.
  • Joint solving beyond about 0.3 m lever arms, and multi-joint drive.
  • Shell with through holes.
  • Arcs on the legacy profile path.
