# Robonine analytic joint fixture

This is an imported-parts, kinematic solver fixture. It does not establish physical fit, collision-free travel, gear coupling, contact, dynamics, or native editable feature history.

The exact XBF assets and source provenance are pinned by `analytic/observations.json` and its README. Cylindrical rail face `0:1:1:1:9` in part 020 (SHA-256 `932cc0452078850d9059ed6ad0716dd3658d35b8117c5598c2f0699e2b14b21c`) measures radius 2.85 mm, axis +X on the line through `(24.888015895285545, -16, 11)` mm. Cylindrical bore face `0:1:1:1:6` in part 040 (SHA-256 `2e780afa05c59f10fb3a8c4acf1cc0c2289d45312dedc0172bc7fa83ecbeffc0`) measures radius 3 mm, axis -Z on the line through `(0, 0, 6.202235200000838)` mm. These are same-kernel OCP 8.0.1.1 measurements of retained XBF source labels, not independent metrology or STEP entity addresses. Joint frames are queried and certified by the production worker; rooted side A is an explicit literal datum equal to the initial world composition of side B, not a frame attached to the grounded geometry.

The two slider bounds [-42, 0] mm are test authoring bounds: the nominal x=+42 mm jaw plane and its root-Z-pi copy at x=-42 mm are 84 mm apart, yielding 42 mm per side under symmetric closing. This does not establish supplier-certified travel, simultaneous contact-free motion, or physical end stops. Gear bounds [-pi, pi] use the solver's supported principal-angle interval, not physical stops. The rack-clamp relationships are authored rigid pairs. Both sliders and the pinion are driven independently; no gear relation is claimed.

OCP Boolean diagnostic samples on the exact source parts show overlap at clamp-only -1 mm against a stationary rack (8.892 mm³), at rack-only -1 mm against a stationary gear (12.2693698698312 mm³), and at clamp-only +1 mm against the frame (247.97871327906375 mm³). These samples do not characterize continuous travel. Do not use the numerical solver test bounds as collision-free or supplier travel qualification.

The workflow also exports the nondefault solved pose through the public STEP service. The assembly export is flattened in millimetres; it does not carry the authored joint model or hierarchy. The test reimports it into a fresh OneCAD runtime and checks six body meshes against source-derived posed bounds with the existing 0.15 mm tessellation envelope. This second read uses the same worker and is not an independent-kernel check.

For a retained STEP and independently invoked OCP 8.0.1.1 reader comparison, set `ONECAD_ROBONINE_EXPORT_OUT` to a new `.step` path when running the test. The test refuses to overwrite the retained STEP or its `.oracle.json` manifest. Run `export_check.py STEP SOURCE_DIR NEW_REPORT.json` with the pinned analytic XBF directory as `SOURCE_DIR`. It requires six valid solids, millimetre units, and a bijective match of source-BRep-derived posed bounds within 0.001 mm, fixed before the comparison. Adaptive and nonadaptive volume, centroid, area, Boolean validity, and volume conservation are diagnostics; they do not imply independent metrology or a mass tolerance. OCP and the worker use the same OCCT family.

The sampled comparison's clamp Boolean common operations did not conserve volume exactly (maximum reported discrepancy approximately 0.0013378659 mm³). Treat those Boolean values as diagnostics, not proof of volume equivalence. The checker uses separate volume and surface accumulators for clarity. A direct OCCT probe confirmed that the earlier shared accumulator was overwritten by the surface calculation; both checker revisions produced identical reports, including area and centroid values.
