## Decisions

- **D1 Sketch host-face tracking mirrors the mate reseat exactly** (worker resolves at the `Sketch` step, echoes `planStep.sketchPlacement`, Rust adopts as a derived no-undo writeback inside `finish_regen`; unresolvable ⇒ §9 `NeedsRepair` on the sketch step, frozen plane stands, never dropped, never silently moved). Rejected: Rust-side two-phase regen (breaks the single `ExecutePlan` transaction and checkpoints); FE-side re-projection (FE is never a geometry authority).
- **D2 A Cut that removes no volume is REFUSED**, symmetric with the existing Add-disjoint refusal (`OP_FAILED`, reasonCode `CUT_NO_EFFECT`, evidence `{volumeBefore, volumeAfter, tolerance}`); WP-D1 isolation keeps unrelated bodies. Rejected: warning-only (the review's minimum; inconsistent with the Add precedent and with "a failed feature must not report success").
- **D3 `SketchUpsert` reports solved `positions`/`curves` (additive) and Rust adopts them into the document on every upsert, inside the SAME undo entry as the edit that triggered it** (refresh-only upserts adopt as their own entry, "Resolve sketch"; sketch sessions already squash to one net step at exit). Consequence recorded: a legacy document whose authored geometry violates its inferred H/V moves onto the constraints on first open — that is the constraints finally being true. Rejected: keeping the identity solve and only reporting residuals (the extruded solid would stay wrong).
- **D4 Residual gate becomes a decision** (the SCHEMA §7.4 pre-announced follow-up): a converged solve whose per-constraint residual exceeds its own-dimension tolerance reports `Conflicting` with `conflicting` = the violating constraints (named culprits). Tolerances and the angular scaling law come from Astra A2; `solverPolicyVersion` bump ruled by the protocol-auditor.
- **D5 Drag policy = drive-only**: the dragged point is the only tag(−1) drive, every committed constraint stays active, nothing else is pinned; PlaneGCS solves from the current pose (FreeCAD/SolidWorks behaviour). `on_end` derives status from a fresh unpinned exact solve after the drive is released. Group drag unchanged. Rejected: repairing the rectangle strategy (a heuristic that only ever worked for identity-shared corners).
- **D6 Semantic redundancy dedupe at commit**: an authored `Perpendicular`/`Parallel` intent is dropped when both lines are axis-locked (directly or through the H/V/Parallel/Perpendicular direction chain — the union-find R5 rule lifted out of `mockConflicts.ts` into a shared pure module). Shapr3D refuses redundant adds; we never author them.
- **D7 Trim remaps, never silently drops**: Radius/Diameter carry to every surviving arc piece; positional relations remap to the piece that keeps the position; the rest are dropped WITH a hint naming them. Fillet authors `Radius` (typed value) + endpoint tangency (WP-3b). Deleting a fillet arc restores the corner when both welded legs are tangent to it and non-parallel (Shapr3D/Fusion behaviour).
- **D8 Snap feedback has one writer**: `SketchController` publishes the per-frame `SnapDecision` (or null) to `viewportStore`; the indicator, the badge and the status-bar readout all read it; the raw raycast readout is the fallback only when no decision exists. Invariant (tested): indicator ≡ last published decision or hidden.
- **D9 Edge picking arbitrates in screen space with a depth tolerance scaled by pixel size at the hit depth** (zoom- and FOV-invariant), replacing the world-distance bias. Fillet/Chamfer enable for a FACE selection (expanded to that face's edges at arm); body selection stays disabled with the reason "Select edges or a face" (user, 2026-09-14).
- **D10 Default camera: perspective, FOV 35°, same three-quarter iso framing** (user). Fit centres in a symmetric chrome-inset rect (not the largest clear rectangle). Face-sketch entry pads 1.6× and drops the plane tint to 0.22. TOP of a WORLD plane shows world +X right / +Y up (camera only; persisted bases untouched) and the readout labels X/Y there.
- **D11 Visibility toggles leave the undo stack** (user): `SetVisibility` becomes a view-state transaction that records no undo entry; still saved with the document. Recorded as a user-visible decision in `TODO.md`; `e2e/model-undo.spec.ts`/`tree-visibility.spec.ts` expectations change with it.
- **D12 Re-editing an Extrude exposes end condition, symmetric and boolean mode**; changing to/from NewBody may cascade `NeedsRepair` downstream — deterministic, acceptable, hinted.
- **D13 User-facing error layer**: `EngineError::OpFailed` Display = the message only (code/recoverable stay structured); `toClientError` stops prefixing the kind; the status bar truncates to one line with the full text on hover; region hashes never reach a sentence (reasonCode copy table).
- **D14 Contracts:** `INSPECTOR_SECTIONS_CONTRACT.sketchMode` gains an "Entity" section (S12) — a recorded user-visible decision, not a probe fix. No other contract edits; `modelingInteraction.golden` must stay green untouched.
- **D15 Astra pre-approved** (user): A1/A2/A3 `derive` at xhigh, one `break` at xhigh after each kernel/identity diff (WP-1, WP-2, WP-3); fan-out ≤2; grounded on ≤12 named paths; no `ultra`. Each run's approval summary goes in the Run log.
- **D16 Commits** (user): one commit per green gate boundary on `master`, never push; ledger (`TODO.md`) in the same commit. Five commit boundaries: WP-0 · WP-1+WP-2 · WP-3 · WP-4+WP-5 · WP-6/7/8.
