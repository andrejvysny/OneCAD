A. Executive verdict

OneCAD has a substantial correctness framework, but this commit has reproducible failures in geometry execution and reference lifecycle. Two of the original three P1 findings survive. The third conflates directed joint frames with contact mating.

The most consequential independently verified results are:

  • Near-axis Revolve terminates the production worker with SIGSEGV. This also occurs through the current version-3 sketch-region path.
  • A rename during prepared regeneration supersedes the result without scheduling replacement work. Authoritative history and accepted geometry can remain at different stages.
  • Two-direction ToFace can publish a valid solid on the wrong side. Geometric validity does not detect the violated direction contract.
  • Reference evidence and bindings have distinct lifecycle defects. Promotion can be overwritten by regeneration; rollback to zero destroys durable evidence; a completed rebind is not re-armed after a later full replay.
  • A rotated Gear rejects a newly picked legitimate cap. Its referenceability classifier still uses the original gear axis.
  • The assembly solver has real convergence limitations, but the executed successful solves satisfied independently recomputed constraints. Movement of an undriven joint was permitted free mobility, not a constraint failure.

The unchanged C++ suite produced 244 passes and three failures in this Linux build. All six required backend tests passed serially. The failed Boolean-internals assertions are a qualification discrepancy that must be investigated; they are not proof that invalid geometry was published.

This is not a production-readiness certification. It establishes specific failures, specific counterexamples to the first audit, and bounded positive evidence. Native macOS/Metal and iPad execution remain outside this Linux review.

E. Rejected or overstated findings

Free mobility is not a failed drive

The source explicitly defines joint value as a rest/drive target enforced only when driven. The native panel says âRest / drive valueâ and has a separate drive checkbox. In the independent two-slider example, driving J2=5 permits B1ââ0.5 and B2â4.5 with one remaining DOF. Driving J1=2 as well gives B1â2, B2â7 and zero DOF. Both satisfy the intended constraints.

Pinning every other joint would remove allowed mobility. Writing measured coordinates over all stored targets would change authored settings. A âhold other coordinatesâ option or measured-coordinate display is a separate product feature. Sources: Joint contract, target selection, panel.

Directed frames are not contact mates

The solver aligns world attachment axes. Two outward normals pointing in opposite directions therefore require a relative flip under that authored contract. A mathematically satisfied flipped pose is not evidence of wrong residuals. What is missing is a persisted user modifier for flip/offset when authoring geometry-bound joints. Add that capability relative to the resolved base datum; do not weaken source-identity validation.

Likewise, a plane datum may be outside its trimmed face, and a cylindrical joint datum should lie on the axis. Requiring every origin to lie on the picked face or replacing every origin by a centroid is unsuitable. Joint semantics, picked-origin construction, classification.

The read ONECAD-530 and ONECAD-533 explicitly bound the work to grounded trees and independently driven joints. They do not promise contact, collision, coupled mechanics or dynamics.

Boolean and hole semantics require the correct oracle

  • Union with a contained tool can correctly leave geometry unchanged. Legacy one-tool consumption is specified; newer array-form Boolean already checks participation.
  • Counterbore construction is the union of two tools. A deeper, wider tool contains the drill. Adding a depth-order restriction requires an explicit compatibility decision.
  • A hole may intentionally break out through an edge. Full containment within the selected face is not the contract. Connected-host validation is a separate condition.
  • Add on admitted legacy multi-solid aggregates does expose a documentation/lifecycle inconsistency, but that does not prove corrupted solid geometry.

Sources: Boolean contracts, Hole tools, publication policy.

The ignored-test gate exists at the report baseline

The same-commit native gate documentation, host gate implementation, backend test list, and workflow explicitly enumerate and run the six ignored backend tests with --ignored --nocapture --test-threads=1. Recommending creation of that serial gate overlooks existing implementation. Historical concurrent failures remain unverified; they do not establish that production workflows must be serialized the same way.

For original34, the real-worker save/reopen probe retained a 25 mm mesh while history requested 50 mm. On fresh reopen it reported geometry_source=cached, with both features Dirty. Cold replay produced a live50 mm mesh and 39,999.99999999999 mmÂ³, with both features Ok.
